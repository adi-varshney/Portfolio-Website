STEMpathAI

My partner and I built STEMpathAI, a STEM career explorer with a 28-question assessment, personalized career matches, and step-by-step roadmaps. We placed third at the New Jersey TSA State Conference and qualified for nationals.

Built with Python and Flask, the app uses SQLite to save accounts, quiz results, and roadmap progress. Its interface uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to make career paths easy to explore.
